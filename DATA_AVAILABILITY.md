# Data availability text for the manuscript

## Current public statement

The reproduction code, complete compressed input data, validation outputs, and exact final figure artwork are publicly available at https://github.com/jindudkjy/DAC-coverage-representation and archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24617626. The hourly meteorological inputs were obtained from the NASA POWER hourly point API; the repository retains the exact temperature, relative-humidity, solar-irradiance, and wind records used in the analysis, with checksums and source provenance.
